## 🎯 BAGIAN 1: CONTOH SOAL UTS (STATIVE VERBS, TENSES & READING COMPREHENSION)

### Soal UTS 1: Stative vs Dynamic Verbs & Error Identification
**Deskripsi Soal:**
Pilihlah bagian kalimat yang salah (berisi error gramatika) dari kalimat-kalimat teknis komputasi berikut, lalu berikan pembetulannya!

1. *"At the moment, the lead software architect is not believing that the distributed database can handle one million requests per second."*
2. *"The new encryption library is consisting of several cryptographic modules designed for zero-trust networks."*

**Kunci Pembahasan & Solusi:**
1. **Analisis Kalimat 1:**
   * **Bagian Salah:** `is not believing`.
   * **Penjelasan:** Kata kerja `believe` (percaya/berkeyakinan) merupakan **Stative Verb** yang menyatakan kondisi kognitif/mental (*mental state*), bukan aksi fisik. Stative verbs tidak boleh digunakan dalam bentuk *progressive / continuous* (`-ing`).
   * **Pembetulan yang Benar:**
     > *"At the moment, the lead software architect **does not believe** that the distributed database can handle one million requests per second."*
2. **Analisis Kalimat 2:**
   * **Bagian Salah:** `is consisting of`.
   * **Penjelasan:** Kata kerja `consist of` (terdiri dari) adalah **Stative Verb** yang menyatakan komposisi/kepemilikan relasional (*relational state*).
   * **Pembetulan yang Benar:**
     > *"The new encryption library **consists of** several cryptographic modules designed for zero-trust networks."*

### Soal UTS 2: Reading Comprehension Teks Teknis IT (Soal Asli UTS Undip)
**Teks Bacaan:**
> *"Cybersecurity researchers have recently uncovered a sophisticated strain of ransomware targeting cloud-native environments. Unlike traditional malware that encrypts local hard drives, this variant exploits compromised API keys to exfiltrate and subsequently delete cloud storage buckets. The attackers then demand payment under the threat of releasing sensitive enterprise data to public repositories. Analysts emphasize that enforcing multi-factor authentication (MFA) and least-privilege access policies remains the most effective defense against such lateral movement."*

**Pertanyaan:**
1. What is the primary distinction between the new ransomware variant and traditional malware mentioned in the passage?
2. What does the term **"exfiltrate"** in line 3 most closely mean?
   * (A) To decrypt systematically
   * (B) To illegally transfer or steal data
   * (C) To execute background processes
   * (D) To compress storage files
3. Which mitigation strategy is recommended by security analysts?

**Kunci Pembahasan & Solusi:**
1. **Perbedaan Utama:** Traditional malware targets and encrypts local hard drives, whereas this new variant targets cloud-native environments by exploiting compromised API keys to steal and delete cloud storage buckets.
2. **Arti Kata "Exfiltrate":** Jawaban yang benar adalah **(B) To illegally transfer or steal data**. Dalam terminologi keamanan siber (*cybersecurity terminology*), *data exfiltration* mengacu pada penyalinan, pemindahan, atau pengambilan data secara tidak sah dari sistem korban ke server penyerang.
3. **Strategi Mitigasi:** Enforcing Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and implementing least-privilege access policies.

## 🎯 BAGIAN 2: CONTOH SOAL UAS (CONDITIONAL SENTENCES & PASSIVE VOICE)

### Soal UAS 1: Conditional Sentences (Soal Asli UAS Informatika Undip)
**Deskripsi Soal:**
Lengkapilah kalimat-kalimat pengandaian berikut dengan bentuk kata kerja yang tepat sesuai dengan tipe conditional sentence yang ditentukan!

1. *If the network administrator ________ (monitor) the firewall logs regularly, he ________ (detect) the data breach before it occurred.* *(Type 3 - Past Unreal)*
2. *If quantum computers ________ (become) commercially accessible today, our current RSA encryption algorithms ________ (be) obsolete.* *(Type 2 - Present Unreal)*
3. *If a programmer ________ (not allocate) memory properly in C, a segmentation fault ________ (occur).* *(Type 0 / 1 - General Fact)*

**Kunci Pembahasan & Solusi:**
1. **Kalimat 1 (Third Conditional - Peristiwa Masa Lalu):**
   * Formula: *If + Past Perfect ($had + V_3$), would have + $V_3$*.
   * Jawaban: **had monitored**, **would have detected**.
   * *"If the network administrator **had monitored** the firewall logs regularly, he **would have detected** the data breach before it occurred."*
2. **Kalimat 2 (Second Conditional - Hipotesis Masa Sekarang):**
   * Formula: *If + Simple Past ($V_2$), would + $V_1$*.
   * Jawaban: **became**, **would be**.
   * *"If quantum computers **became** commercially accessible today, our current RSA encryption algorithms **would be** obsolete."*
3. **Kalimat 3 (Zero Conditional - Fakta Teknis Ilmiah Pasti):**
   * Formula: *If + Simple Present, Simple Present*.
   * Jawaban: **does not allocate**, **occurs**.
   * *"If a programmer **does not allocate** memory properly in C, a segmentation fault **occurs**."*

### Soal UAS 2: Transformasi Kalimat Aktif ke Pasif dalam Abstrak Ilmiah
**Deskripsi Soal:**
Ubah kalimat-kalimat aktif informal berikut menjadi bentuk kalimat pasif akademik formal (*Academic Passive Voice*) yang lazim digunakan dalam abstrak skripsi/jurnal ilmiah informatika:

1. *"We tested the proposed convolutional neural network on a benchmark dataset containing 50,000 labeled images."*
2. *"The database engine automatically indexes incoming transactions to minimize query latency."*

**Kunci Pembahasan & Solusi:**
1. **Transformasi Kalimat 1:**
   * Objek aktif: *the proposed convolutional neural network*.
   * Bentuk Tense: Simple Past (`tested` $\rightarrow$ `was tested`).
   * **Bentuk Pasif Akademis:**
     > *"The proposed convolutional neural network **was tested** on a benchmark dataset containing 50,000 labeled images."*
2. **Transformasi Kalimat 2:**
   * Objek aktif: *incoming transactions*.
   * Bentuk Tense: Simple Present (`indexes` $\rightarrow$ `are indexed`).
   * **Bentuk Pasif Akademis:**
     > *"Incoming transactions **are automatically indexed** by the database engine to minimize query latency."*

## 🎯 BAGIAN 3: SIMULASI UJIAN LISAN KOMPREHENSIF (*ORAL DEFENSE*)

### Q1: "Mengapa penggunaan kalimat pasif (Passive Voice) sangat dominan dalam penulisan laporan teknis dan abstrak skripsi Informatika berbahasa Inggris?"
**Jawaban Komprehensif:**
Dalam penulisan ilmiah (*scientific writing*), fokus utama laporan adalah pada **objek penelitian, fenomena, metodologi, dan data hasil eksperimen**, bukan pada diri peneliti secara personal.
Penggunaan kata ganti orang pertama seperti *"I"* atau *"We"* (misal: *"We trained the model"*) cenderung subjektif dan informal. Mengubahnya menjadi bentuk pasif (*"The model was trained"*) memberikan nada objektif, netral, dan mengedepankan integritas metodologis dalam komunitas akademik internasional.

### Q2: "Jelaskan apa yang dimaksud dengan Stative Verbs dan berikan contoh penerapannya dalam terminologi perangkat lunak!"
**Jawaban Komprehensif:**
*Stative verbs* adalah kata kerja yang merefleksikan keadaan tetap (*state of being*), persepsi, kepemilikan, atau proses mental abstrak, dan bukan suatu tindakan fisik yang memiliki durasi progresif. Oleh karena itu, kata kerja ini tidak lazim digunakan dalam bentuk kontinu (*continuous / -ing*).
Contoh dalam terminologi perangkat lunak:
* Kata `contain`: *"The array contains 10 elements"* (Benar) $\rightarrow$ bukan *"The array is containing"*.
* Kata `depend`: *"Module A depends on Module B"* (Benar) $\rightarrow$ bukan *"Module A is depending on"*.
* Kata `belong`: *"These permissions belong to the root user"* (Benar) $\rightarrow$ bukan *"are belonging"*.
